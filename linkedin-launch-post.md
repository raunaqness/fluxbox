# FluxBox LinkedIn Launch Post

I've used a bunch of little tools over the years — free ones, paid ones, scattered across the menu bar and the browser.

Currency converter here. Market tickers there. Timers in another app. System stats somewhere else. Ambient sounds in yet another tab.

I got tired of the fragmentation. So I built the thing I actually wanted: **one app that combines all of them.**

**FluxBox** is an ultra-lightweight macOS productivity command center.

Hit **Alt + Space**, and everything is right there:

→ Currency & unit converters
→ Quick timers
→ Ambient sounds
→ Recent apps
→ Live stocks & crypto
→ World clocks + weather
→ RAM / disk / network stats

Built with **Rust + Tauri** — fast, tiny footprint, stays out of the way.

Try it: https://fluxbox.raunaqness.com/

Feature requests welcome — if there's a tool you still juggle separately, tell me. That's exactly why I built this.
